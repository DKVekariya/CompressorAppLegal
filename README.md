## ✨ Features

### 🎬 Compress Video
- Choose a **quality level** (Low / Medium / High) or set a **target file size**
- Cap the **resolution**: Original, 4K, 1080p, 720p, or 540p (only sizes that make sense for your video are offered)
- Save as **MP4** or **MOV**
- Option to keep the video's **metadata**
- See an **estimated size** before you start

### 🖼️ Compress Image
- Pick a **quality level** or aim for a **target file size**
- Output as **JPEG, PNG, or HEIC**
- Optionally limit the **maximum dimension** to shrink large photos
- Option to keep the photo's **metadata**

### 🔄 Convert Media
- Convert images between **JPEG, PNG, and HEIC**
- Convert videos between **MP4 and MOV**
- Open **WebP, AVIF, and camera RAW** photos and convert them to a format that works everywhere
- Open **MKV** videos and convert them to MP4 or MOV (supported MKV files)

### 📁 My Media
- All your compressed and converted files in **one organized place**
- Browse by type (Videos / Images) and by action (Compressed / Converted)
- Switch between **grid and list** views, **select multiple files**, **share**, or **delete**
- **Storage** screen with a breakdown by category, so you can clear space with one tap
- Save your results to **Photos** when you want them in your library

### ⚙️ Made for you
- **Light, Dark, or System** appearance
- **English and Hindi** (हिन्दी)
- Quick onboarding you can skip
- Saved presets so you don't repeat the same settings
- Works on **iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision**

## ❓ FAQ

**Does the app upload my photos or videos?**
No. All compression and conversion run locally. The app doesn't need an internet connection to do its work.

**Will it change or delete my originals?**
No. Results are saved as new files, so your originals stay exactly as they are.

**Why is the result not exactly my target size?**
Target-size mode searches for the best quality that fits, and results are approximate. Some files simply can't reach a very small target without unacceptable quality. Try a slightly larger target or a lower resolution.

**Where are my compressed files?**
In **My Media** inside the app. They can also be saved to Photos, and appear in the Files app (iOS/iPadOS) or Finder (Mac) under the app's folder.

**A file didn't convert. What now?**
Some unusual formats or codecs aren't supported. Please [open an issue](../../issues/new/choose) and tell us the file type and device, and we'll look into it.

**Is it free?**
See the App Store listing for current pricing.
