# Privacy Policy

**CompressorApp**
**Effective date:** October 2, 2026
**Last updated:** October 2, 2026

CompressorApp ("the App", "we", "us") lets you compress and convert videos and images. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses and how it is handled.

## The short version

- **Everything happens on your device.** Your photos and videos are processed locally and are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
- **We collect nothing.** No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
- **We have no servers** that receive your data, because the App does not connect to the internet to do its work.

## 1. Information we collect

**None.** We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The developer cannot see your files, your settings, or how you use the App.

## 2. Information the App accesses on your device

The App only accesses data on your device to do the job you ask for:

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Photos and videos** you choose from your Photos library or Files | To compress or convert them | Processed on-device only |
| **Photos library (add-only)** | To save compressed or converted results back to Photos when you choose to | Written to your own Photos library |
| **Files you open or import** | To compress or convert them (for example, MKV videos) | Processed on-device only |
| **App preferences** (appearance, language, onboarding status, compression presets) | To remember your choices | Stored locally on your device |

### Photos permission
The App asks for permission to read your Photos so you can pick items to process, and to add results to your Photos. You choose which items to select. You can change or revoke this at any time in **Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos** (iOS/iPadOS/visionOS) or **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos** (macOS). If you deny access, you can still use Files import where available.

## 3. Your files and where they are stored

Compressed and converted files are saved in the App's own storage on your device, in a folder called "App Media" (organized into Videos/Images and Compressed/Converted). Temporary working files are created during processing and removed afterwards.

- The App **does not modify or delete your original photos or videos**. It creates new files.
- You can view, share, and delete the App's files from inside the App, and manage space under **Settings > Storage**.
- The App enables **file sharing**, so these files may also be visible to you in the Files app (iOS/iPadOS) or Finder (macOS).
- Deleting the App removes its stored files and preferences from your device. Files you saved to your Photos library or exported elsewhere are not deleted.

## 4. Sharing of information

We do not share information with anyone, because we do not receive any. If you choose to share a result using the system share sheet (for example, sending it by Messages or saving it to a cloud service), that action is handled by the app or service you choose and is governed by their privacy policies.

## 5. Third-party services

The App contains **no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs**.

The App is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple may collect information about downloads and, if you have opted in on your device, anonymous diagnostic and usage data that it may make available to developers. This is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and your device settings, not by us.

If you use iCloud or device backups, the App's files and preferences may be included in those backups according to your device settings.

## 6. Legal links inside the App

The Privacy Policy and Terms of Use links in Settings open this website in your browser. Visiting the page is subject to the hosting provider's own practices (GitHub Pages); the App itself sends no information when you tap them.

## 7. Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It is suitable for general audiences.

## 8. Security

Because your media never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security (passcode, Face ID/Touch ID, encryption). We recommend keeping your device updated and locked.

## 9. Your rights

Under laws such as GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since we do not collect or hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide or delete. You control all data directly on your device.

## 10. International users

Because no data is transferred to us, there are no cross-border data transfers.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do, and material changes will be reflected in a new version of the App or on this page. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

## 12. Contact us

Questions or concerns about this policy? Open a request on our support page:

**[github.com/DKVekariya/CompressorApp/issues](https://github.com/DKVekariya/CompressorApp/issues)**

Please note that GitHub issues are **public**. Do not include personal information, photos, videos, or other private files in your message. If you need to share something private, say so in the issue and we will arrange another way to continue.
